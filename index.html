How The Vault Works

In three simple steps, a fully automated, on-chain reward cycle.

01

Reward Claim

The system automatically claims the accumulated pump.fun creator reward into the treasury wallet.

02

Swap To $Jimothy

The claimed reward is swapped into $Jimothy, preparing the vault balance for the next distribution round.

03

Automatic Payout

The swapped $Jimothy is automatically distributed in batch transactions directly to eligible holders' wallets.
